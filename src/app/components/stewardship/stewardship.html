A Global Legacy Worth Preserving

For more than 180 years, cooperatives have demonstrated that enterprise can be built on democracy, solidarity and shared prosperity. Across every continent, cooperative buildings, archives, museums, educational institutions, enterprises and living traditions tell the remarkable story of communities working together for the common good.

The Cooperative Cultural Heritage initiative exists to identify, document, safeguard and promote these tangible and intangible expressions of cooperation. By preserving this heritage, we strengthen cooperative identity, inspire future generations and demonstrate the continuing contribution of cooperation to sustainable development, peace and social justice.

This is an invitation to help preserve one of humanity's most important social and economic, living movements of people, by people and for people & our planet

Why Become a Steward of Cooperative Heritage

Your stewardship enables the identification of new heritage sites, documentation of endangered customs & traditions that embody cooperation, digitization of ICA archives, educational resources and leadership journeys for young people and worldwide recognition of the cooperative movement.

As stewards, you will be recognized at the highest settings in the cooperative world, as international leaders in preserving a shared legacy that belongs to cooperators everywhere.
