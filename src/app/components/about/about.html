About the Cooperative Cultural Heritage Programme

The ICA Cooperative Cultural Heritage Programme celebrates the world's cooperative story from historic buildings and monuments to traditions and practices that bring people together. These are the places and ideas that have stood the test of time, inspiring solidarity, trust, and shared purpose across generations.

Born in the United Nations International Year of Cooperatives, this global initiative is uncovering and honouring the most inspiring examples of cooperative heritage - the landmarks and living traditions that remind us what people can achieve when they work together.

The definition of cooperatives according to the ICA Statement on the Cooperative Identity, recognizes the ability of members to meet their economic, social and cultural needs and aspirations through their cooperatives, and the CCH Programme initiates a concrete process which aims to bring together the enterprise of 1 billion co-operators in meeting their cultural goals.

Programme Goals

  • To systematically identify, document, and safeguard the tangible and intangible manifestations of cooperative values and principles across continents.
  • To build a purposeful journey for youth, exchanges among the people of various cooperative regions worldwide.
  • To establish a Cooperative Cultural Heritage Inscription in recognition of the cooperative movement's enduring legacy and its unique contribution to global human history.

This ambitious undertaking by the ICA establishes a Cooperative Cultural Heritage Inscription in recognition of the cooperative movement's enduring legacy and its unique contribution to global human history and through its continuing service, to sustainable development.

The ICA Cooperative Cultural Heritage Programme was launched in the United Nations International Year of Cooperatives (2012) to celebrate and preserve the cooperative heritage that has shaped communities worldwide.

The programme is guided by a dedicated working group that brings together experts from various cooperative sectors, cultural heritage organizations, and international bodies to ensure comprehensive and authentic representation of cooperative heritage.

This collaborative approach ensures that the programme reflects the diverse expressions of cooperative culture across different regions and communities, while maintaining the highest standards of cultural preservation and recognition.

What is Cooperative Cultural Heritage?

Cooperative Cultural Heritage encompasses both tangible and intangible elements that embody the cooperative spirit. Tangible heritage includes physical sites, buildings, archives, and infrastructure associated with cooperative history. Intangible heritage comprises traditions, governance systems, cultural values, and collective practices that underpin cooperation and are passed down through generations.
